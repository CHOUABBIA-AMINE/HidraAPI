## Concrete fail-closed, rollback and race assertions

- PlanTarget's empty-policy/legacy-mapping case aborts migration without fabricating
  mappings, then permits a reviewed fixture retry. Wrong-family, incomplete value,
  orphan/cross-revision and optional-parent cases preserve their actual semantics.
- PlanTarget's committed nomination reparent and scenario deletion cases hold an
  opposing transaction before a waiting fresh write; they assert the expected
  integrity denial. These are controlled local race examples, not load testing.
- Monitoring's deletionCommittedFirstDeniesConcurrentEvaluationReference and
  deviationCommittedFirstDeniesConcurrentEvaluationReassignment exercise both
  mutation orders. directJpaSaveRejectsUnknownOwnerEvidenceAndRollsBackFlushedWrites
  and liveRecordUsesSpringTransactionRetainsFallbackAndRollback cover distinct paths.
- Nomination's actualOwnerProvidersSaveCanonicalPartyAndRollbackTheFlushedRecord,
  inactiveUnchangedHistoryAndSnapshotsReplayButFreshReferencesFailClosed and
  duplicateCodeRaceHasExactlyOneWinner assert provider, history and transaction
  behavior under their exact fixture. Eligibility/parent locks are separate cases.
- [DocumentContentTransferServiceTest](../../src/test/java/dz/sh/hidra/modules/documents/application/service/DocumentContentTransferServiceTest.java) invokes synchronization outcomes:
  confirmed rollback cleans newly created bytes; committed/unknown preserves
  possibly referenced content. This unit fixture is distinct from real database
  transaction/storage acceptance and from authorized historical disposal.
